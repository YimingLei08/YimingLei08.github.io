About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Civil & Mineral Engineering at the University of Toronto , working with Prof. Giovanni Grasselli. My research develops efficient continuum–discontinuum numerical methods for deep rock mechanics and rock engineering, together with data-driven and multi-scale models of rock deformation and failure.

I received my Ph.D. in Geotechnical Engineering from Wuhan University in 2025, advised by Prof. Quansheng Liu. My doctoral research developed a three-dimensional GPU-parallelized FDEM framework and coupled it with multibody dynamics to investigate TBM–rock interaction and segmental lining failure.

Research Interests

  • Computational rock mechanics
  • Tunnelling engineering, Tunnel boring machine (TBM)
